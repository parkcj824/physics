## What would be needed before a claim

An eligible future candidate needs a computational rule that is concretely different from existing kernel, spectral, Galerkin, graph, structured-dynamics and learned-solver operations; a valid mathematical statement; positive evidence on heterogeneous heat **and** nontrivial fluid momentum; a speed/data/accuracy advantage against strong appropriate controls including setup cost; and verified full-paper plus patent prior-art review. A new name or different physical narrative is insufficient.

Potential future subject matter is limited to **unrun questions**, not invention proposals: endpoint-integrable response changes without hidden inverse solves; constraint witnesses that bound a learned response rather than recreate all solver iterations; and orientation-sensitive response objects that retain transport direction while respecting balance. Each has substantial potential prior-art overlap and must first be screened. No claim language, priority assertion or patentability advice is supplied.
